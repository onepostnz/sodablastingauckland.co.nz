What Is Sandblasting?

Sandblasting vs Soda Blasting

Sandblasting

Sandblasting is a process of erosion that uses a pressurized stream of abrasive particles — sand, metal shot or glass beads — to remove material from a surface. It's used for cleaning, stripping, polishing and smoothing surfaces across many industries, and is typically used for larger jobs such as metal fabrication or automotive restoration.

Soda Blasting

Soda blasting uses sodium bicarbonate (baking soda) to clean various surfaces. It's an effective, safe way to remove paint, rust, stains and other contaminants from metal and other materials without damaging them — environmentally friendly, gentle on surfaces, fast and cost-effective.
