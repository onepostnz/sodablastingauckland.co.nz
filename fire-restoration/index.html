Fire Restoration Auckland

If you're looking for a fast, efficient way to restore your fire-damaged property, Auckland Soda Blasting Pro is here to help — removing soot, smoke damage and other residue with our soda blasting technology.

Get A Free Quote Call 09 888 0623

Our process uses advanced soda blasting technology to remove soot, smoke damage and other residue from affected surfaces, restoring many items damaged by fire back to their pre-fire condition.

Our fire restoration service also includes stabilisation of the environment where the incident occurred, reducing airborne and surface contamination levels quickly so the area is safe for any occupants.

What's Included

  • Restores brickwork, masonry and timber
  • Removes smoke damage from walls and ceilings
  • Non-abrasive, won't damage underlying surfaces
  • No harsh chemicals or solvents required
  • Stabilises the environment after the incident
