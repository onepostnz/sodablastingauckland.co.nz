Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Auckland Soda Blasting Pro (“we”, “us”, “our”, “this website”) is a lead generation and referral website. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect through this website, how we use it, and your rights in relation to it, in accordance with the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020.

What We Collect

When you submit a quote request or contact form on this website, we may collect:

  • Your name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Property address
  • Details of the service you're enquiring about and preferred timeframe
  • Any photos or plans you choose to upload

We do not require you to provide more information than is necessary to respond to your enquiry.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Pass your enquiry to a local, independent contractor who can contact you about your quote
  • Respond to you directly if you contact us
  • Improve this website and the services referred through it

We do not use your information for any purpose beyond connecting you with a contractor and following up on your enquiry.

Who We Share It With

As a referral website, the core purpose of collecting your details is to pass them to a contractor who carries out the work. We do not sell your personal information to third parties, and we do not share it with anyone beyond the contractor fulfilling your enquiry, except:

  • Where required by law
  • With service providers who help us run this website (e.g. hosting, form, and CRM providers), who are only permitted to use your information to help deliver that service

Data Storage & Security

Enquiry information is stored securely within our website and CRM systems. We take reasonable steps to protect your information from loss, misuse, and unauthorised access, but no online transmission or storage system can be guaranteed 100% secure.

Cookies & Analytics

This website may use cookies and analytics tools (such as Google Analytics or Meta/Facebook Pixel) to understand how visitors use the site and to measure the performance of our advertising. This data is generally anonymised or aggregated and is not used to personally identify you.

Your Rights

Under the Privacy Act 2020, you have the right to:

  • Ask what personal information we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct any information that is wrong
  • Ask us to delete your information, where reasonably practicable

To make a request, contact us using the details below.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, you can contact us at:

info@sodablastingauckland.co.nz
