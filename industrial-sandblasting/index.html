Industrial Sandblasting

Industrial Sandblasting Auckland

At Auckland Soda Blasting Pro, we offer industrial sandblasting services to meet all your needs - rust removal, paint stripping, graffiti removal and surface restoration on old structures or brand new ones.

Get A Free Quote Call 09 888 0623

Industrial sandblasting is an effective way to clean and prepare surfaces for various applications. It removes dirt, paint, rust, corrosion and scale from a range of materials, while creating a smooth finish that's ideal for painting and coating.

We'll work with you to determine the right abrasive media for your project — from fine glass bead media for delicate surfaces to coarse steel grits for tougher cleaning jobs — so you get the desired results every time.

What's Included

  • Rust, paint and corrosion removal from a range of materials
  • Smooth surface finish, ideal for painting or coating
  • Abrasive media matched to your job for consistent results
  • Cost-effective, low-impact alternative to chemical stripping
  • Experienced technicians, right the first time
