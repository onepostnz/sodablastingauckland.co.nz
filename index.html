Soda Blasting vs Sandblasting

Our Sandblasting & Soda Blasting Services

Soda blasting uses crushed soda pellets as an abrasive instead of traditional materials like sand or glass beads. It's ideal for removing paint and varnish, degreasing machinery, and removing graffiti — all without damaging the underlying surface.

Sandblasting uses more traditional abrasives such as sand or glass beads, propelled with high pressure air to remove old paint, rust, grime or etching. It allows us to clean large areas quickly while maintaining full control over the level of abrasion.
